About the Porsche 924 Le Mans Edition

The Porsche 924 Le Mans Edition is a special version of the Porsche 924, created to commemorate Porsche's success at the 24 Hours of Le Mans. This limited edition model features unique styling cues, enhanced performance, and exclusive interior details that set it apart from the standard 924.

Under the hood, the 924 Le Mans Edition is powered by a 2.0-liter inline-four engine, delivering a thrilling driving experience. The car's lightweight design and balanced chassis make it a joy to drive on both the road and the track.

With its distinctive black and white livery, the 924 Le Mans Edition is a true collector's item, representing a significant chapter in Porsche's racing history.

Specifications

  • Engine: 2.0-liter inline-four
  • Power: 125 PS (92 kW)
  • Top Speed: 201 km/h
  • 0-100 km/h: 9.5 seconds
  • Transmission: 5-speed manual
  • Weight: 1134 kg
  • Production Year: 1980
